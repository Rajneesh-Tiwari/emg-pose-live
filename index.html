Pipeline

What goes in, what comes out, and how it is scored.
Data

emg2pose (Salter et al., NeurIPS 2024): 193 people, roughly 370 hours of 16-channel wrist sEMG at 2 kHz, with motion-capture hand pose as 20 joint angles. We use the official splits and train on the train split only.

Validation

Held-out people × held-out movement types. Every architecture, hyperparameter and A-vs-B choice is made on validation alone. The test sets are reported next to it and never selected on.

Windows

5 s scored per window, preceded by 0.9 s of causal context so the recurrent state is warm before the first scored sample. Input scaling matters here: EMG is kept in its physical units throughout.

Training

AdamW, warm-up then cosine over 150 epochs, loss = L1 on joint angles plus a forward-kinematics fingertip term (Hadidi et al., 2026). We start from that paper's defaults and change a setting only on our own measured evidence.

Run 2 adds

Multi-task training — the Tracking and Regression protocols share one set of weights — and heavy EMG augmentation: channel dropout, time masking, per-electrode gain and noise perturbations, and band rotation.

Evaluation

Pooled mean absolute angular error in degrees, exactly as Codabench computes it: absolute error over every window, joint and time step. Our local scorer is verified against the leaderboard — 15.6115 locally vs 15.611 returned.

Runs

One fixed seed (42) throughout; no multi-seed averaging.
